Scoring Reference

  • Single 1 = 100, single 5 = 50.
  • Three 1s = 1,000. Three 2s-6s = face value × 100.
  • Each extra matching die doubles the three-of-a-kind value.
  • Straight 1-6 = 1,500.
  • Three pairs = 1,500.
  • Two triplets = 2,500.
  • Six of a kind = 3,000.
  • No scoring dice after a roll = Farkle. Turn points are lost.
  • If all six dice score, you get hot dice and roll all six again.

Game Log

Official-Style Farkle Rules

Roll six dice. After each roll, keep at least one scoring die. You may bank your turn points or risk them by rolling the remaining dice. If all dice score, all six dice become available again as hot dice. First player to reach 10,000 triggers the final round so both sides get equal turns.
