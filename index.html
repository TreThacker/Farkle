How to Play

Roll six dice. After each roll, select scoring dice by clicking them. Click a selected die again to deselect it before rolling or banking. Turn Points previews your selected dice before they are banked. You may then roll the remaining dice or bank your turn points. If you roll no scoring dice, that is a Farkle and you lose the points for that turn.

Scoring Reference

  • Single 1 = 100 points.
  • Single 5 = 50 points.
  • Three 1s = 1,000 points.
  • Three 2s-6s = face value × 100.
  • Each extra matching die doubles the three-of-a-kind value.
  • Straight 1-6 = 1,500 points.
  • Three pairs = 1,500 points.
  • Two triplets = 2,500 points.
  • Six of a kind = 3,000 points.
  • If all six dice score, you get Hot Dice and may continue your turn by rolling all six dice again.

On the Board

Each player must score at least 500 points in a single turn before making their first banked score. This is called getting On the Board. Once a player is on the board, future banks may be any valid score.
