## 🎯 Objective

Be the first player to reach **10,000 points**.

Once a player reaches 10,000 points, the **Final Round** begins and each player receives the same number of turns. The highest score wins.

---

## 📖 How To Play

1. Roll all six dice.
2. Select scoring dice.
3. Choose whether to:

   * Roll remaining dice
   * Bank your points
4. Avoid a Farkle.
5. Reach at least **500 points in a single turn** to get **On The Board**.
6. Reach 10,000 points before your opponent.
